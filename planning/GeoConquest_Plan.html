Daily Quests

3 Duolingo-style quests a day, one easy, one medium, one hard. The hard one's chest is the daily chest.

Designed
▾

Decisions

  • Remove the daily leaderboard (Daily tab, #1 prize, its server logic). Leaderboard keeps Weekly + All-time.
  • No more reward just for logging in.
  • "Daily Quests" section with a reset countdown; each quest has a progress bar and a chest.
  • Each quest is a template with a changeable number → easy / medium / hard versions. New quest types = new template, no new code per quest.
  • Quests may overlap (like Duolingo), but never two from the same template on one day.
  • Never give a quest the player can't do (no Nightmare without the Dark Scroll, no Millionaire with 0 tickets).
  • Hard quest ≈ 15–20 minutes of play at most. First 3 days: easier versions. Favour modes the player hasn't played lately.
  • Templates, numbers, weights and rewards live in the database, editable from the dashboard.
  • Accepted: XP/gold targets scale with player level · 1 free swap a day · reset at midnight UTC · old app versions stop getting the old login reward.

Weekly Challenge

Global competition, percentage-based so new players can beat veterans.

Designed
▾

Decisions

  • Each week = one format + one random quiz mode, e.g. "Speed & Accuracy · Capitals".
  • Random rotation; last week's format and mode can't repeat.
  • Principle: everyone does the same task and is ranked against each other. Anything about your own history belongs in Daily Quests.
  • One global board, no leagues. Week = Monday 00:00 → Sunday 23:59 UTC.
  • Auto rotation with dashboard override. New "Weekly" tab on the Leaderboard.
  • Millionaire / Nightmare in rotation, with a free entry that week. Same Quiz Sprint played at Medium.

Passport

Replaces Home + Quests. Collect country stamps at a rare 0.3% chance per correct answer. Wonders tab teases the endgame.

Designed
▾

Layout

  • Tabs: Passport · Quizzes · Shop · Leaderboard.
  • Header: avatar, flag, name, level ring, gold. Tap avatar → profile menu / Sign Out.
  • Subtabs: Main (conquest map + Daily Quests + progress, opens by default) · Stamps · Quests · Collection (owned avatars, upgrades and backgrounds; equip here) · Wonders (teaser with unlock progress until unlocked).
  • Top Explorers mini-leaderboard dropped.

Stamps

  • Purpose: extend completion, a long-term collection. Rare on purpose.
  • 0.3% per correct answer, flat for every country, adjustable in the dashboard. No guarantee / pity timer.
  • Per question, in every mode: a correct answer about the US can drop the US stamp.
  • No duplicates: owned stamps never drop again.
  • ≈ 1 stamp per 33 quizzes at first, slower as the book fills.
  • Art: the flag recoloured in red ink inside two red circles, slightly rotated, faded edges. No per-country art.
  • Moment: a small "Stamp found!" chip mid-quiz; full stamp-slam animation on the results screen.
  • Share a passport page as an image.

Later

  • Stamp milestone quests · stamp sound effect.

From your notes

  • Profile/user menu moves behind the avatar in the Passport header: shorter rows, smaller icons.
  • Collection must list avatars from every source (shop, quests, daily chest, milestones, wonders). Fixes: chest avatars missing from Owned.
  • Check the 'Game Master' quest icon when Quests move into the Passport.
  • Flags as collectibles live in Passport > Collection with avatars and backgrounds (see C7).

Economy Config in the Dashboard

Every price, reward and level requirement editable without an app update.

Designed
▾
Security hotfix first. When buying a country or an avatar, the app tells the server the price and the server trusts it. A modified app could buy for 0, or send a negative price and gain unlimited gold. Level requirements are also only checked in the app. Fix: the server works out prices and checks levels itself.

Design

  • game_config table (key, value, category, description, min, max) + a change history table.
  • App loads settings at launch and on return to the app; built-in defaults as fallback.
  • Server reads prices and rewards itself for every purchase and reward, including quiz gold/XP.
  • New "Game Config" dashboard tab: grouped, inline edit, min/max checks, confirm summary, history.

Editable

ShopTicket + pack prices, country price formula + per-country overrides, avatar / upgrade / crate prices, crate odds
QuizzesGold + XP per correct answer per mode, difficulty multipliers, ticket cost
LevelsMinimum level per avatar, upgrade, quiz mode, quest (enforced on the server)
Daily QuestsRewards per tier, chest contents, templates
WeeklyPrizes, participation reward, enabled formats
StampsDrop chance (0.3%)
Real moneyGold per store pack
OtherReferral bonus, Conqueror multipliers

From your notes

  • Country price x5 / minimum 200 becomes a dashboard value (see conflict C2).
  • Mythic chest odds stored in config; the server rolls with the same numbers the shop shows, so stated and real chances can't differ.
  • Potions included in server-side reward calculation, applied last; same-tier XP potions stack.
  • Mythic chest / gold pack rebalance and bulk-buy tiers (x1 / x5 / x10) priced from the dashboard (see C3).

Mainland Outlines + Missing Shapes

No more tiny dots for France, and no more "?" instead of a shape.

Designed
▾

Overseas territories

  • Automatic rule drops far parts: France (118° wide → 14°), Netherlands, Portugal, Spain, Chile, Ecuador, Australia, South Africa, NZ, Mexico, Japan, Yemen.
  • USA: keep Alaska, drop Hawaii and the date-line Aleutians. Russia: drop the date-line part, keep Kaliningrad.
  • Norway: drop Svalbard. India, Malaysia (both halves), Fiji and Pacific archipelagos: keep as is.
  • Exclaves always kept: Kaliningrad, Cabinda, Nakhchivan, Musandam, Bioko.
  • Antarctica: shown from above the South Pole, like on its flag, not the stretched flat-map version.

Why "?" appears

  • 15 countries aren't in the map file: Tuvalu, Gibraltar, Tokelau, Kosovo, French Guiana, Réunion, Guadeloupe, Martinique, Mayotte, Caribbean Netherlands, Christmas Is., Cocos Is., Bouvet Is., US Minor Outlying Is., Svalbard & Jan Mayen.
  • Kosovo has no ID in the file.
  • The shape view downloads the world map from the internet every time, although it's already inside the app.
  • ~54 more have very rough outlines (Vatican, Monaco, Singapore, Malta, Hong Kong…).

Fix

  • One detailed shape file inside the app for every country (Natural Earth, free), looked up by country code, mainland rules applied.
  • One contact sheet of all ~250 shapes for a visual check.
  • Anything still unusable: kept out of shape questions and shown as a flag, never a "?".

From your notes

  • Missing shapes, USA / New Zealand / Russia fixes, the same shapes used in Learn, Antarctica from the South Pole.
  • Learn uses the new bundled shapes: slower zoom, move / zoom the mini map; Learn button in every regular quiz except Borders.

Endgame: Wonders

After level 100 and every country owned: paint world wonders by earning their pigments.

Designed
▾

The problem

  • After level 300 there's nothing new; a player who played hours every day hit 300 and stopped.

Decisions

  • Unlock: level 100 + every country in the shop owned (~1.5M gold today; ~7.5M if country prices go ×5).
  • The conquest map is replaced by a painting of a world wonder in 10 colours. It starts empty: locked colours are hidden (not grey), so the finished picture is a surprise.
  • Each colour (pigment) comes from a mega quest: a list of requirements turned in together, e.g. "Borders 90%+ ×30" + "500,000 gold". Gold is spent on turn-in.
  • Several mega quests can progress at once. Requirements mix modes so it doesn't feel like one long grind.
  • Pigment Trial dropped (it would eat the gold the quests need).
  • 5 wonders at launch, more added over time. Each wonder is our own pixel art, drawn by a script with exactly 10 colours (no copyright issue).
  • Finishing a wonder: title, avatar frame, badge on profile and leaderboard, shareable painting, and the wonder becomes an avatar background.
  • Passport "Wonders" tab is visible to everyone as a teaser, with live progress ("Level 64 / 100", "Countries 112 / 250").

Wonder art & reveal system Assets built, screen not yet

  • Each pigment unlocks a random colour of that wonder (not chosen by the player, not one fixed order). Not every colour has to be a meaningful step.
  • One image per wonder: 1600×1200, exactly 10 colours, no blending. Keep the subject in the centre (~75% wide, ~80% tall): narrow phones crop the sides.
  • Shown in the same card as the world map on Home: 260 high, width = screen width − 66 (about 294–364), cover-fit.
  • Files per wonder in assets/wonders/<id>/: final.png (master) + layer_0…9.webp (one transparent 800×600 lossless WebP per colour; layers never overlap, so any order works).
  • scripts/wonders/build_layers.py makes the layers and app/lib/wondersData.ts from final.png + wonders.json; it refuses anything that isn't exactly 10 colours. wonderReveal.ts picks a random locked colour.
  • ~190 KB per wonder, so 10 wonders are under 2 MB. ~19 MB memory only while one wonder's 10 layers are on screen.
  • Built: all 10 wonders (art, layers and data file), ~2.1 MB in total: Giza, Taj Mahal, Great Wall, Machu Picchu, Petra, Colosseum, Christ the Redeemer, Angkor Wat, Ha Long Bay, Victoria Falls. Art scripts live in scripts/wonders/art/.

The 10 wonders (agreed; launch with 5)

  • In drawing order, each with its own mood: Great Pyramid of Giza (Egypt, golden-hour sunset, art done) · Taj Mahal (India, pre-dawn) · Great Wall of China (misty dawn) · Machu Picchu (Peru, sunrise) · Petra (Jordan, red canyon) · Colosseum (Italy, blue-hour dusk) · Christ the Redeemer (Brazil, above the clouds) · Angkor Wat (Cambodia, sunrise over a lotus pond) · Ha Long Bay (Vietnam, golden sunset) · Victoria Falls (Zambia / Zimbabwe, mist + rainbow; border: owner = whoever holds Zambia, or a special case).
  • Chichén Itzá was swapped out: too close to Giza (another stepped pyramid). Uluru replaced it first but was dropped after the art review; Ha Long Bay (Vietnam) took its place. No Oceania wonder for now.
  • Good launch picks (easiest to recognise): Taj Mahal, Christ the Redeemer, Angkor Wat, Ha Long Bay, + Great Wall or Machu Picchu (see W4).

Wonder questions to decide before building the screen

  • W1 Applying a pigment: the random colour appears at once on mega quest turn-in, or the player taps "apply"? Suggestion: tap to apply, short animation.
  • W2 The random pick should happen on the server (like the gold fix) so nobody can re-roll. Suggestion: table user_wonders (user, wonder, unlocked-colours bitmask 0–1023, completed_at) + server function grant_pigment.
  • W3 Faint colours: the Giza navy and dark brown are almost invisible on the dark card, so those unlocks look like nothing happened. Accepted for now; later a lighter backdrop (~#16162E) or the random pick avoids two faint colours in a row. Check each new wonder.
  • W4 Which 5 of the 10 launch, in what order? Add an Oceania wonder?
  • W5 Exactly 10 mega quests per wonder (1 pigment each)? How cost grows from the first to later wonders.
  • W6 Finished wonder as avatar background (Feature 13): we need the background crop shape / ratio to check every wonder still works cropped. The share image can reuse final.png.
  • W7 Locked teaser in the Wonders tab: "greyed / silhouetted" no longer applies (locked colours are hidden). Empty card with the name, a darkened final image, or an outline?
  • W8 After finishing: a gallery of finished wonders, and what the card shows before the next wonder starts.
  • W9 Reveal animation for a new colour (fade in only that layer, maybe a flash) and a "6/10 colours" label.

Endless level milestones

  • After 300: a milestone every 50 levels, forever (350, 400, 450…).
  • Most pay big gold + tickets; every 3rd (450, 600, 750…) gives an exclusive avatar or background.
  • Assign new avatars/backgrounds to future milestones from the dashboard; empty ones pay gold.

Other endgame ideas (not chosen now)

  • Seasons · Country Mastery · Endless mode · Leagues · Duels with friends.
