FEATURE 1: DAILY QUESTS  (DESIGNED)
------------------------------------------------------------

DECISIONS
  - REMOVE the Daily Leaderboard entirely (Daily tab, the #1 daily
    prize, and its server logic). Leaderboard keeps: Weekly + All-time.
  - REMOVE the "reward just for logging in". Nothing is granted for
    opening the app anymore.
  - ADD 3 daily quests per player, Duolingo style:
      * "Daily Quests" section with a countdown to reset
      * One row per quest: title, progress bar (e.g. 20 / 20), chest icon
  - Each quest has a reward chest. The HARDEST quest's chest is the
    "Daily Chest" (the reward that used to come from logging in).
  - Quests may overlap in objective (e.g. "Earn XP" and "Complete 3
    quizzes" fill together), like Duolingo.
  - Every quest is a TEMPLATE with a number that can change, so one
    template gives an Easy, Medium and Hard version. New quest types =
    one new template, not new code per quest.
  - Note: the newest app version (with the daily chest logic) is not in
    this repo, so this is a pure design and will be reconciled with that
    code when implementation starts.

1C. RULES FOR PICKING EACH DAY'S 3 QUESTS
  - One Easy, one Medium and one Hard every day.
  - Overlap allowed, but no two quests from the same template on the
    same day.
  - Never give a quest the player can't do: no Nightmare without the
    Dark Scroll, no Millionaire with 0 tickets, no locked modes.
  - Time limit: the Hard quest should take about 15-20 minutes of play
    at most.
  - Bias toward neglected modes: favour modes the player hasn't played
    lately (helps discovery).
  - Ease in new players: for their first 3 days, only easy-ish versions.
  - Weights and amounts live in the database: every template, its
    numbers per tier, how often it's picked, and its reward are stored
    in a table, editable / switchable from the admin dashboard.


1D. REWARDS
  - Easy chest: a little gold.
  - Medium chest: gold + XP.
  - Hard chest: the Daily Chest (keeps the escalating 7-day cycle,

  - Conqueror subscribers: 3x on the Daily Chest (moved from the old
    login reward); update Premium screen text.
  - Opening animation: tap chest -> it shakes and opens, rewards fly
    out, confetti + reward sound.

FEATURE 2: WEEKLY CHALLENGE  (DESIGNED)
------------------------------------------------------------
Requirement: must be PERCENTAGE / result based, not totals, because
"most XP" or "most gold" gives high-level players a huge edge.

DECISIONS
  - Each week = one FORMAT + one random QUIZ MODE
    (e.g. "Speed & Accuracy - Capitals").
  - Random rotation. Last week's format can't repeat; last week's mode
    can't repeat (pushes players into modes they don't usually play).
  - Paid/locked modes (Millionaire, Nightmare) either excluded or given
    a free entry that week.
  - User favourites: Speed & Accuracy, Gold Efficiency.
  - PRINCIPLE: Weekly = global competition, everyone does the SAME
    task, ranked against each other. Anything about your own history /
    personal bests / self-improvement belongs in Daily Quests, NOT here.

FORMATS (all percentage / ratio based, same task for everyone)
  A  Speed & Accuracy (*)  % correct x speed bonus in the week's mode
  B  Gold Efficiency (*)   gold spent / gold earned this week
  C  Consistency           % of this week's quizzes scoring >= 80%
                           (min 5 quizzes)
  G  Lightning Round       % correct among answers given in < 3 seconds
  J  Same Quiz Sprint      identical 20 questions for everyone;
                           ranked by %, then time
  K  Continent Clash       % correct on one random continent, in the
                           week's mode
  L  Hard Countries        % correct on hard-rated countries only
  M  Perfect Rate          % of this week's quizzes that were 100%
                           (min 5 quizzes)
  N  Compass Precision     average error in degrees, lower wins
                           (after Compass mode ships)
  (*) = user's favourites

REMOVED (self-based, moved out of Weekly by decision):
  Level Rush, Quest Hunter, Personal Best, Redemption, Explorer

GOLD EFFICIENCY GUARDRAILS
  - Only gold EARNED THIS WEEK counts (no spending old savings).
  - Gold bought with real money excluded (not pay-to-win).
  - Minimum to qualify (e.g. earn >= 2,000 gold this week).
  - Score capped at 100%; ties -> earliest to reach it.
  - Side benefit: acts as a gold sink for the economy.

SETTINGS (user accepted the suggestions)
  - Launch formats: A, B, C, G, J, K, L, M. N after Compass Quiz ships.
  - Gold Efficiency (B) has no quiz mode.
  - Millionaire / Nightmare are in the mode rotation, with a free entry
    that week.
  - One global board, no leagues for now.
  - Week: Monday 00:00 UTC to Sunday 23:59 UTC.
  - Challenge picked by automatic rotation, with a dashboard override.
  - New "Weekly" tab on the Leaderboard (Leaderboard = Weekly +
    All-time).
  - Format J (Same Quiz Sprint) is played at Medium difficulty unless
    the dashboard says otherwise (Feature 7).

STILL OPEN (settle together with Economy config)
  W5. Attempt limits per format. [Suggested: 1 free attempt + extra
      attempts for a ticket for fixed-quiz formats (J); unlimited for
      average-based formats]
  W6. Prizes for 1st-3rd, participation reward, badge for 1st.
      All amounts set in the dashboard.

FROM YOUR NOTES (merged into this feature)
  - Leaderboard rework: format large XP numbers (1.2M) so they fit.

FEATURE 3: WEEKLY WINNERS ANNOUNCEMENT  (DESIGNED)
------------------------------------------------------------
WHEN
  - Full-screen popup the first time each player opens the app after
    the week closes (Monday 00:00 UTC), once per player.
  - Then stays as a "Last Week's Champions" card at the top of the
    Weekly leaderboard tab until the next results.

CONTENT
  - The challenge: format + mode + one-line description.
  - Podium: 2nd left, 1st centre (raised), 3rd right. Each spot shows
    avatar, name, score in the format's unit, level, prize won.
  - Your own result: "You placed #47 of 1,203 - Top 4%", or "You
    didn't play" + Play button.
  - Teaser for this week's challenge with a "Play now" button.

ANIMATION / SFX
  - Drumroll -> podium blocks rise 3rd, 2nd, 1st -> avatars drop on
    -> confetti for 1st, fanfare, haptic on each reveal. Skip button
    after 1 second.

WINNERS
  - Top-3 players see their own version first ("You won 1st place!")
    with a Claim reward button and a bigger celebration, then the podium.

HOW RESULTS ARE PRODUCED
  - Server job at week end ranks once, saves the result (challenge,
    top 3, player count), pays prizes. Popup only reads the saved
    result (fast, cheat-proof). Replaces the current approach of
    computing the daily winner when someone opens the leaderboard.

DECISIONS
  - One global podium (no leagues).
  - Podium entries show what the home page shows for a player: name,
    avatar, flag, plus score and prize.
  - Share as image: available to EVERYONE ("I placed #47 / Top 4%"),
    with a nicer card for the top 3.
  - Fewer than 3 qualified: show filled places only; skip if none.

LATER (not in this update)

  - Fanfare / drumroll sound effects (animation ships without them,

FEATURE 4: PASSPORT  (DESIGNED)
------------------------------------------------------------
PURPOSE
  Stamps exist to EXTEND the game's completion: a long-term collection
  that keeps players playing. Rare on purpose.

TABS
  Passport | Quizzes | Shop | Leaderboard
  (Passport replaces both Home and Quests)

MAIN PASSPORT PAGE (DECIDED) - opens by default
  - Header: avatar, flag, name, level ring, gold ("passport ID page");
    tap avatar -> profile menu / Sign Out.
  - Content: the conquest map (owned countries / empire progress),
    your Daily Quests, and current progress. That's it.
  - Top Explorers mini-leaderboard is dropped.
  - Subtabs: Main | Stamps | Quests | Collection | Wonders
      Main       - conquest map + Daily Quests + current progress
      Stamps     - passport book, one page per continent, grid of stamp
                   slots (collected = stamp art, missing = faint
                   outline), counter "37 / 250 - Africa 5 / 54"
      Quests     - current Achievements/Quests screen
      Collection - owned avatars + upgrades; avatars are EQUIPPED here
                   (bought in the Shop)
      Wonders    - endgame paintings (Feature 12); teaser + unlock
                   progress until level 100 + all countries owned

STAMP DROP RULES (DECIDED)
  - Chance: 0.3% per CORRECT ANSWER (dashboard-adjustable).
  - Per QUESTION, not per quiz: any correct answer about a country, in
    ANY mode, can drop that country's stamp.
  - No duplicates: once you own a country's stamp, that country can't
    drop again. Only uncollected countries roll.
  - Math check: ~10 correct per quiz -> ~3% per quiz -> about 1 stamp
    per 33 quizzes early on, slowing down as the collection fills.
    Full 250 is a very long-term goal (intended).

STAMP ART (DECIDED - generic, no per-country art)
  - The country's flag, recoloured to red/ink tones (red multiply
    tint over the flag image, so the design stays visible), inside
    two red concentric circles, slightly rotated + faded edges for
    an "inked" look. Optional: country name curved along the ring.
  - Same component for every country; no hand-drawn assets.

STAMP COLLECTED MOMENT
  - Small "Stamp found: Peru!" chip mid-quiz (doesn't pause timer).
  - Full animation on the results screen: passport slides up and
    opens, stamp slams down (squash), ink spreads, slight screen
    shake, heavy haptic. SFX: later.

DECISIONS
  - Flat 0.3% for every country (no rarity tiers).
  - No guaranteed stamp / no pity timer.
  - Share a passport page as an image: yes.
  - Avatars equipped in Collection (inside Passport).
  - Default page: Main.

LATER
  - Stamp milestone quests (10/25/50 stamps, full continent).

FEATURE 5: ECONOMY CONFIG IN THE DASHBOARD  (DESIGNED)
------------------------------------------------------------
!! SECURITY FINDINGS (fix FIRST, as a separate small hotfix) !!
  - purchase_country(p_country_code, p_cost): the APP sends the price
    and the server trusts it. A modified client can buy any country
    for 0 gold, or send a NEGATIVE cost, which ADDS gold (infinite gold
    in one call).
  - purchase_avatar_item(..., p_cost): same issue.
  - Level requirements (avatars like FrogLord lvl 50, Trail Quiz lvl 50,
    etc.) are only checked in the app, not on the server.
  - Hotfix: server computes prices itself (country price from the
    countries table area; avatar price from a server-side price list),
    rejects cost <= 0, and checks level requirements. App stops sending
    prices. (Assumes the repo's migrations match what is live.)
  - Also review purchase_quiz_upgrade and open_crate for the same
    pattern during the hotfix.

DESIGN
  1. game_config table: key, value, category, description, min, max,
     updated_at. Readable by all players, writable only by the
     dashboard (admin key). game_config_history table logs every
     change (old value, new value, time).
  2. App: loads config at launch + on return to foreground, caches it,
     falls back to built-in defaults. All hard-coded numbers replaced
     with config lookups.
  3. Server: every purchase / reward function reads prices and rewards
     from game_config itself and never trusts a number from the app
     (countries, avatars, upgrades, crates, quests, chests, weekly
     prizes, quiz rewards).
  4. Dashboard: new "Game Config" tab - grouped by category, inline
     edit, min/max validation, confirmation summary before saving
     ("Ticket price: 2000 -> 2500"), change history. Daily quest
     templates and weekly formats get their own editable tables here.

EDITABLE SETTINGS
  Shop         ticket price, ticket pack price, country price formula
               (currently max(20, area / 100)) + per-country overrides,
               avatar prices (all in DB -> sales / repricing), upgrade
               prices, crate prices + odds
  Quizzes      gold + XP per correct answer per mode, ticket cost per mode
  Levels (NEW) minimum level for each avatar, each upgrade, each quiz
               mode (e.g. Trail lvl 50), and each quest that has one;
               enforced on the SERVER too
  Daily Quests rewards per tier, chest contents, templates + numbers
  Weekly       1st-3rd prizes, participation reward, enabled formats
  Stamps       drop chance (0.3%)
  Real money   gold amount per store pack (store sets the money price)
  Other        referral bonus, Conqueror multipliers

DECISIONS (user accepted all suggestions)
  - Fix the purchase exploits first as a separate small update.
  - Quiz gold/XP calculated on the SERVER in this update.
  - Country price: formula (divider + minimum) + optional per-country
    overrides.
  - All avatar prices move to the database.
  - Gold amount per real-money pack is editable.
  - Changes apply on next app open / return to foreground.
  - Dashboard stays single-admin with the admin key.
  - Minimum level requirements are editable (user addition).

ACCESS

FEATURE 7: QUIZ DIFFICULTY (Easy / Medium / Hard)  (DESIGNED)
------------------------------------------------------------
REQUEST
  Flags, Shapes, Capitals and Borders get 3 difficulties, picked on
  the quiz's starting page: 1 Easy, 2 Medium, 3 Hard.

WHAT ALREADY EXISTS (app/lib/questionDifficulty.ts)
  - getCountryDifficulty(): scores each country 1-10 (hand-picked
    "iconic" and "well-known" lists, then by population).
  - getSmartDistractors(): harder wrong answers at higher difficulty
    (1-3 random, 4-6 same region, 7-10 same region + similar size).
  - BUT only Millionaire / Nightmare use them. Flag, Shape and Capitals
    quizzes pick countries and wrong answers fully at random.
  - The user's newer version already has a quiz starting page and no
    "Easy Flags" card.

DESIGN
  Two levers per difficulty:
    a) WHICH countries are asked (by the 1-10 score)
    b) HOW CLOSE the wrong answers are
  Easy    countries scored 1-4, wrong answers random from the world
  Medium  countries scored 1-7, wrong answers from the same region
  Hard    mostly countries scored 6-10, wrong answers same region +
          similar size; Flags also use a hand-made list of look-alike
          flags (Chad/Romania, Indonesia/Monaco, Ireland/Ivory Coast..)
  - quiz_results gets a difficulty column (needed for daily quests like
    "score 85% on Hard" and for weekly challenges).

DECISIONS
  - Quizzes with difficulty: Flags, Shapes, Capitals AND Borders.
    (Borders: same two levers - which countries are asked, and how
    close the wrong answers are, e.g. Hard asks about small/obscure
    neighbours.)
  - Rewards: MEDIUM = today's normal gold + XP (x1, matches today's
    random quizzes). Easy x0.75, Hard x1.25. Both multipliers editable
    in the dashboard, AND the base gold + XP per correct answer for
    each quiz is editable there too (Economy config -> Quizzes).
  - Hard is open to everyone, any time (no level lock).
  - Starting page already exists in the user's newer version. Add the
    1 / 2 / 3 picker + Start button there.
  - The app remembers the last difficulty per quiz and pre-selects it,
    but the starting page is ALWAYS shown so the player confirms.
  - Per-country difficulty (the 1-10 score) is editable from the
    dashboard, in this update. Needs a country_difficulty table
    (country code -> score) that the app loads like game_config, with
    the current built-in scoring as the fallback.
  - Same number of questions and same timer on all difficulties.

HARD MODE: 6-ANSWER QUESTIONS (user addition)
  - In Hard, some questions randomly show 6 answers instead of 4.
  - Marked with orange + purple borders so the player notices.
  - Layout: 3 x 2 grid of answers; must fit small phones.
  - Frequency: 40% of Hard questions (editable in the dashboard).
  - No bonus reward: a 6-answer question pays the same as any other.
  - Orange/purple on the question card's border, with a small
    "6 OPTIONS" label (suggested; easy to change).
  - Only when 6 valid answers exist. All Borders questions are
    multiple choice, but some can't have 6 options (e.g. a question
    built from a country's neighbours when it only has 3). In that case
    the question stays at 4 answers. Same fallback rule for every quiz.

FEATURE 8: DAILY CHALLENGE  (in design)
------------------------------------------------------------
DECISIONS
  - Name: "Daily Challenge" (the old daily leaderboard is gone, so
    the name is free).
  - 3 questions a day, the same for everyone, reset at midnight UTC:
      Q1 Palette, Q2 Zoom, Q3 Neighbours.
  - Answers are TYPED (with autocomplete + forgiving spelling), not
    4 options.
  - Timed: about 20 seconds per question.
  - Leaning: one guess per question ("take it or leave it").
  - Must NOT block normal daily rewards: Daily Quests and the daily
    chest stay reachable for players who do badly here.
  - Unique reward per question (details later).
  - Twins (near-identical flags) DROPPED: too few pairs for a big,
    long-running pool.

Q1 PALETTE
  - Pie chart of a flag's colours; colours at 1% or less grouped into
    "Other".
  - Data: one-time script reads every flag image, groups pixels into
    standard colours, saves colours + percentages per country into a
    data file shipped with the app. One manual review pass.

Q2 ZOOM
  - A country's outline, zoomed far in on part of its border/coast.
    Mockup: planning/daily_zoom_mockup.png (drawn for multiple guesses;
    with one guess, the map instead zooms out slowly during the 20s,
    so answering earlier is harder but could pay more).
  - Start zoom tight (~20-25x) on a less iconic stretch.
  - Country pool: large to normal countries only. Israel, Qatar,
    Bahrain OK; San Marino, Vatican, Singapore, Hong Kong, small
    islands OUT - the map data has too little detail for them.
    Area alone doesn't work as the cutoff (Hong Kong is bigger than
    Bahrain), so use area + how detailed the outline is in the map
    data, then one manual review of the list.

Q3 NEIGHBOURS (DECIDED)
  - Show the target country's neighbours, each in its OWN separate
    tile (like the Shape Quiz), not on a map. Player types the
    country they all border (France, Germany, Austria, Italy,
    Liechtenstein -> Switzerland).
  - Mockup: planning/neighbours_mockup.png (A = shapes, B = flags).
  - DECIDED: alternate by day - shape days and flag days. On shape
    days, tiny neighbours still fall back to their flag.
  - Uses the app's existing border data; islands / countries with
    fewer than 2 neighbours excluded. Cap the tiles at ~6 for
    countries with many neighbours (China has 14).
  - Shape tiles show the MAINLAND only: overseas territories (French
    Guiana, Reunion...) would otherwise shrink France to dots.
  - Neighbours too small for a clear shape (Liechtenstein, Monaco,
    Vatican...) show their flag instead.

FEATURE 12: ENDGAME - WONDERS  (DESIGNED)
------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM
  After level 100 little happens; quests every 50 levels up to 300,
  then nothing. A very active player (hours a day) hit level 300 and
  stopped playing.

OPTIONS ON THE TABLE
  1. Prestige ("Ascension") - optional reset at level 300 back to 1,
     keep everything owned; earn a prestige rank (shown next to the
     name), an exclusive avatar/frame per rank, small permanent bonus.
  2. Country Mastery - every country levels up Bronze -> Silver ->
     Gold -> Diamond from correct answers across all modes. ~250
     countries x 4 tiers = a very long tail. Builds on the Passport
     (stamp first, then mastery).
  3. Seasons - monthly season track (e.g. 50 tiers) with season-only
     avatars/frames; resets every month, so it never ends. Free track
     + Conqueror track (also revenue).
  4. Endless mode - play until 3 mistakes; global best-run leaderboard.
     Skill has no ceiling.
  5. Empire upgrades - spend gold to develop owned countries (map
     look, titles, small bonuses). A gold sink that fits "Conquest".
  6. Leagues with promotion/relegation (Duolingo style) - later, once
     there are enough active players.
  7. Async duels vs friends - strongest for retention but the biggest
     build; later.

USER'S DIRECTION: "WONDERS" (pigments + paintings)
  - After level 300 + owning every country, the world map is replaced
    by a new stage: a painting of a world wonder, in ~10 colours
    ("pigments"), starting empty (UPDATED: locked colours are hidden,
    not grey - see WONDER ART & REVEAL SYSTEM below).
  - Each pigment is earned, then applied: that colour fills in on the
    painting. All pigments -> wonder complete -> next wonder.
  - Path A, Mega Quest: a list of requirements turned in together,
    e.g. "Borders 90%+ x30" + "500,000 gold" + "30 tickets".
  - (Path B, Pigment Trial - dropped, see below.)

ASSESSMENT
  Strong: fits the game (colours/flags/palettes), very visual progress
  (paint-by-numbers reveal), a real gold + ticket sink for rich
  endgame players, and new wonders can be added forever.
  Watch-outs:
  - Gate too narrow: level 300 + ALL countries means almost nobody
    reaches it. Suggest the first wonder unlocks earlier (e.g. level
    100) and later wonders need higher levels / more countries.
  - Only infinite if content keeps coming: make it cheap - mega quests
    reuse the daily quest templates with big numbers; paintings are
    turned into 10-colour regions by a script. Art must be
    public-domain or our own (no copyrighted photos).
  - "Same quiz 30 times" can feel like a chore: mix requirements
    across modes and let several mega quests progress at once.
  - Gold / tickets in a mega quest should be CONSUMED on turn-in
    (that's what makes it a sink).
  - Pigment Trial: losing many tickets for nothing is harsh. Failed
    tries should still leave something (pigment fragments / a counter
    toward a guaranteed pigment).
  - Replaces Prestige as the post-300 goal; Seasons / Country Mastery
    can still come later.

DECISIONS
  - Unlock: BOTH level 100 AND owning every country in the shop
    (250 countries, about 1.5 million gold in total at today's
    prices - a real long-term goal, and the conquest map is "done").
  - New Passport subtab: Main | Stamps | Quests | Collection | Wonders.
  - Wonders tab is visible to EVERYONE from day one as a teaser:
      * Locked: the wonders shown greyed / silhouetted, with the unlock
        requirements and live progress ("Level 64 / 100",
        "Countries 112 / 250") and a short explainer of what Wonders are.
      * Unlocked: wonders in progress (painting + pigments collected)
        and completed wonders.

MORE DECISIONS
  - Pigment Trial DROPPED: it would burn the same gold / tickets the
    mega quests need. Pigments come from MEGA QUESTS only.
  - Mega quests cost gold, used up on turn-in (gold sink).
  - Several mega quests can progress at the same time.
  - 5 wonders at launch, more added over time.
  - Finishing a wonder gives: a title, an avatar frame, a wonder badge
    on profile / leaderboard, a shareable image of the painting, AND
    the wonder becomes an AVATAR BACKGROUND.
  - Avatar system upgrade: profile = avatar + flag + BACKGROUND.
    Finished wonders are the first backgrounds. Full design: Feature 13.
  - No hard level cap today; after level 300 nothing new happens
    (level quests exist only at 150 / 200 / 250 / 300).

ENDLESS LEVEL MILESTONES (DECIDED)
  - After level 300, a milestone quest every 50 levels, forever
    (350, 400, 450, ...), generated automatically.
  - Rewards spread out so new avatars aren't needed every time:
      * most milestones: a big gold reward (+ tickets), growing a bit
        each time
      * every 3rd milestone (levels 450, 600, 750, 900...): an
        exclusive avatar or avatar background
  - Milestone rewards live in a dashboard table: you can assign an
    avatar / background to any future milestone whenever you have one
    ready; milestones with nothing assigned pay gold by default.

WONDER ART & REVEAL SYSTEM  (ASSETS + DATA BUILT, SCREEN NOT BUILT YET)
  Changes to the design above (DECIDED):
  - Locked colours are HIDDEN (transparent), not grey. A wonder starts
    as an empty card and the finished picture is a surprise.
  - Each pigment unlocks a RANDOM colour of that wonder (not chosen by
    the player, not one fixed order for everybody).

  Art rules (DECIDED)
  - One image per wonder: 1600x1200 with EXACTLY 10 colours (no
    blending, no anti-aliasing). Pixel-art style, drawn by a script, so
    it is our own art (no copyright problem).
  - Keep the subject in the centre (about 75% wide, 80% tall): the card
    crops the sides on narrow phones.
  - Not every colour has to be a meaningful step; some reveal little.

  Where it shows (DECIDED)
  - The wonder takes the same space as the world map card on Home:
    260 high, width = screen width - 66 (about 294-364 on phones),
    shown "cover" (fills the card, crops a little).

  How it works (BUILT)
  - Files per wonder, in assets/wonders/<id>/:
      final.png                 hand-made master, the source of truth
      layer_0.webp ... layer_9  one transparent 800x600 lossless WebP
                                per colour. Layers never overlap, so
                                they stack in any order.
  - scripts/wonders/build_layers.py turns final.png into the 10 layers
    and generates app/lib/wondersData.ts (needed because the app
    bundler only accepts literal require() calls). It refuses an image
    that is not exactly 1600x1200 with 10 colours.
  - scripts/wonders/wonders.json lists the wonders (id, name, country).
  - app/lib/wonderReveal.ts: pickNextLayer(unlocked) returns a random
    colour the player does not have yet (null when all 10 are done).
  - Size: about 190 KB per wonder (master + layers), so 10 wonders are
    under 2 MB. Memory: about 19 MB, only while all 10 layers of one
    wonder are on screen.
  - To add a wonder: put final.png in assets/wonders/<id>/, add a line
    to wonders.json, run:  python3 -I scripts/wonders/build_layers.py
  - Built: ALL 10 wonders (art + layers + wondersData.ts), about 2.1 MB in
    total. Great Pyramid of Giza, Taj Mahal, Great Wall of China, Machu
    Picchu, Petra, Colosseum, Christ the Redeemer, Angkor Wat, Ha Long Bay,
    Victoria Falls. Art scripts are in scripts/wonders/art/ (one per wonder).

  The 10 wonders (AGREED; the plan launches with 5) - drawn in this
  order, each with its own mood so the set doesn't repeat:
     1 Great Pyramid of Giza  Egypt      golden-hour sunset   (art DONE)
     2 Taj Mahal              India      pre-dawn, reflecting pool
     3 Great Wall of China    China      misty blue-green dawn
     4 Machu Picchu           Peru       sunrise, green terraces, mist
     5 Petra (the Treasury)   Jordan     red canyon, beam of sun
     6 Colosseum              Italy      blue-hour dusk, glowing arches
     7 Christ the Redeemer    Brazil     above the clouds, Rio lights
     8 Angkor Wat             Cambodia   sunrise over a lotus pond
     9 Ha Long Bay            Vietnam    golden sunset, karst towers, red-sailed junks
    10 Victoria Falls         Zambia/Zimbabwe  mist + rainbow (hardest;
                              border: owner = whoever holds Zambia, or a
                              special case)
  Chichen Itza was swapped out: it is another stepped pyramid (too close
  to Giza). Uluru replaced it first but was dropped after the art review;
  Ha Long Bay (Vietnam) took its place. No Oceania wonder for now.
  Easiest / most recognisable, good launch picks: Taj Mahal, Christ the
  Redeemer, Angkor Wat, Ha Long Bay, + Great Wall or Machu Picchu (see W4).

  QUESTIONS TO DECIDE BEFORE BUILDING THE SCREEN
   W1. Applying a pigment: does the random colour appear immediately on
